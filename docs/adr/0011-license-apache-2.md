# ADR-0011: Apache-2.0 licence

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-19

## Context
The goal is adoption by businesses, agencies and developers, and contributions from them. Copyleft (AGPL) protects a future hosted business but slows adoption inside companies.

## Decision
All packages, including the dashboard, are Apache-2.0. Contributions use the DCO (`Signed-off-by`), not a CLA.

## Consequences
Anyone, including competitors, may host Ringbridge. Any future commercial offering must win on operations, support and hosted convenience, not licence restrictions.
